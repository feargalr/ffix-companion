# Disc 1 content verification

_Current as of the October 2026 audit. Target platform: Nintendo Switch (also running on Switch 2)._

The full audit is in **[DISC1_AUDIT.md](./DISC1_AUDIT.md)**. It covers individual corrections with evidence, the Chocograph table, the complete ATE inventory, missability totals, image permissions and open questions. This page summarises the rules the dataset follows.

Related files:
- [`content-status.md`](./content-status.md): per-area counts (generated by `npm run report`).
- [`evidence/disc1-evidence.json`](./evidence/disc1-evidence.json): machine-readable record of every entry, its verification state and the source sections used.

Four independent walkthroughs (Jegged; bover_87 and vinheim on GameFAQs; lylat & ponkberry on Steam), plus these dedicated guides:

- the Final Fantasy Wiki’s Chocograph page;

- Fishy071’s and Red Scarlet’s Chocograph guides;
- bover_87’s sidequest pages (Mognet, Kupo Nuts, Stiltzkin, Stellazzio, Chocobo Hot & Cold).

URLs are listed in `src/data/sources.ts` and in the audit.

## Verification states

| State | Meaning | UI |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | Two or more independent sources agree | “Cross-checked” in the details |
| `single-source` | One authoritative source, not contradicted | “1 source” chip |
| `conflicting` | Sources disagree on a material point; the entry’s notes say how | “Sources disagree” chip |
| `unverified` | Not yet checked (none at present) | “Unverified” chip |

Agreement between guides is not treated as proof on its own. Every conflict found was read and written into the entry.

## Missability and priority

- **missability**: `permanent`, `conditional`, `temporary`, `unconfirmed` (no source says whether you can come back) or `none`.
- **missableKind** says what is lost: a `unique` reward, a common `pickup`, a `steal`, an `event`, or an `opportunity`. A chest disappearing is never reported as the item being gone forever.
- **priority** (`critical` / `important` / `optional`) follows the consequence of missing something, not its category. It can be set per entry and is otherwise derived in `src/lib/priority.ts`. Before You Leave shows Critical and Important; everything else is one tap away.

## Open questions

These are kept in sync with the audit’s “Unresolved” list:
- whether Burmecia’s chests and Cancer coin survive later visits (two sources say yes, one says no);
- whether the Ice Cavern can be re-entered on Disc 2;
- the exact cut-offs of three Lindblum ATEs;
- the Autograph/Moogle Suit deadline (single source);
- Mosco’s letter dependency;
- the Grotto Moogle’s name;
- the Friendly Mu location;
- the exact on-screen title of the synthesis tutorial ATE.

Revisit windows for Alexandria, Dali and Lindblum now come from the PSNProfiles treasure guide (see the audit’s second review).

None of these can be settled from published guides alone. They need in-game checks.
